Understanding counselling

A safe space to be heard

What is counselling?

Counselling is a tool that helps us tackle a wide range of issues such as depression, anxiety, stress, self-esteem, anger, relationships, self-improvement and career growth.

It is built on a professional therapeutic relationship in which a trained counsellor offers a safe, confidential and non-judgmental space for the client to work towards insights and solutions to their problems.

Who is a counsellor?

A counsellor is an empathetic listener who creates a safe space and draws on her theoretical orientation and counselling tools. Together, client and counsellor understand the problem and set goals for therapy.

The client explores and understands their patterns of thoughts, feelings and behaviour, and uses these insights and their own strengths to make the change they want — with more insight into themselves and a better ability to manage difficult emotions.

Our mission

To find the missing ingredients in our mind's awareness, desires, beliefs and actions. Counselling helps bring about change in the ways we act — our coping, decision-making and relationship skills — leading to more responsible, fulfilling lives.

When to seek counselling

  • When you need to deal with an immediate psychological or emotional problem.
  • When you want to explore your inner world for emotional growth.
  • When your capacity to respond to the demands of life feels strained.
  • When your natural support systems are not available.

What counselling is not

  • Giving advice or guidance.
  • Offering instant solutions to problems.
  • Being judgmental.
  • “Just talking”.
  • Imposing the counsellor's values, morals and beliefs on the client.
  • The answer to all human difficulties.

About me

Sheela Mariyam Oommen (Sheela Baji)

I have been working in counselling and social work since 1985. I have worked with the Family Court, Ernakulam; the People's Council for Social Justice, Ernakulam; the Juvenile Justice Board, Ernakulam; and the Child Welfare Committee, Ernakulam. I have wide experience in handling family issues, behavioural problems, adolescent issues and effective parenting.

People need a safe and non-judgmental space to talk about the difficult and unpleasant parts of their lives — to have the awkward conversations that are otherwise held back by fear, and to understand their own motivations, thoughts, feelings and actions without judgement.

As the psychologist Carl Rogers said, people only seriously consider change when they feel accepted for exactly who they are. I aim to hold that space for my clients, so they can see themselves as a whole — including the broken, unpleasant and neglected parts — fully accept themselves, and heal.
